About Me

Driven by curiosity,
guided by purpose

I'm a graduate student at Bard College pursuing an M.S. in Climate Science and Policy (STEM), graduating Spring 2027. My work sits at the intersection of geospatial analysis, environmental justice, and climate resilience — turning complex data into policies that protect vulnerable communities. Since July 2026 I have also worked as a Student Assistant at the New York State Department of Public Service, helping track who benefits from the state's clean energy programs.

Originally from New Delhi, India, I bring a cross-cultural perspective to global sustainability challenges. I'm currently developing Research for Policy Change (R4PC), a sustainability research organization bridging academic insights with real-world policy impact.

"Jugaad"
A Hindi concept meaning innovating with the resources available to make the best of any opportunity. Understanding problems through different perspectives without taking them at face value — this guides my work ethic and pushes me forward to explore new avenues.
